# One-color Thirteen Stars mark with China / US symbols as the yin-yang dots

China symbol in the dark half (drawn orange), US symbol in the orange half (drawn black). Contact sheet: symbols-ideas-sheet.png.

- 1. Zhong and Mei (中 / 美): The two characters of 中美, "China–US". 中 (middle) sits in the dark half and 美 (beautiful, short for America) in the orange half.
- 2. Zhong and US (中 / US): 中 in one half and US in the other. The most direct pairing, readable in both languages at a glance.
- 3. Five Stars and Thirteen Stars: The five-star cluster from China's flag and the 13-star ring from the 1777 American flag, each tucked inside the other's color.
- 4. Plum Blossom and Star: The plum blossom, a long-loved Chinese emblem of resilience, opposite a single American star.
- 5. Lantern and Liberty Bell: A Chinese paper lantern facing the Liberty Bell: a light and a bell, both symbols of celebration and freedom.
- 6. Temple of Heaven and Capitol: Beijing's Temple of Heaven and the US Capitol dome, two landmark buildings, each holding the place of a dot.
- 7. Great Wall and Liberty Torch: A Great Wall watchtower and the Statue of Liberty's torch, the two most recognizable landmarks of each country.
- 8. Seal and Shield: A square Chinese seal carved with 中, opposite the striped shield from the US Great Seal. Two national seals, one circle.
- 9. Ping-Pong and Baseball: Each country's favorite game: a ping-pong paddle (a nod to 1971 ping-pong diplomacy) opposite a baseball.
