MUTUAL RESPECT · 9 logo ideas, round 2

Step one: free Eva

1 Keystone

A stone arch between China and the US. Freeing Eva is the orange keystone: put it in place and the whole bridge holds.

2 Embrace

Two arcs, orange and white, reaching around to form one circle. Two equal partners, with Eva held safe in the middle.

3 Reset

A power and reset button. The relationship needs a restart, and freeing Eva is the press of the button.

4 Puzzle Pieces

Two pieces cut to fit each other exactly. Neither side is complete alone; collaboration is the whole picture.

5 Full Circle

Two arrows chasing each other in a loop: what each side gives comes back. The circle starts moving again with Eva.

6 Ping-Pong

Two paddles and a ball, after the 1971 ping-pong diplomacy that helped reopen China–US relations. A small human gesture can reset things again.

7 Moon Gate

A round Chinese garden "moon gate" with a path running through it. An open doorway, and the way forward starts there.

8 First Step

A staircase up to respect. The first step, in orange, is freeing Eva; trust and respect are built on it.

9 人 (People)

The character 人, "person", drawn as two strokes leaning on each other: orange and white, neither standing alone. Respect between people comes first.
