# Thirteen Stars: 9 variations

Chosen direction: yin-yang inside the 13-star ring. Contact sheet: stars-ideas-sheet.png.

- 1. Classic: The chosen idea, refined: an orange and white yin-yang inside the 13-star ring of the 1777 Betsy Ross flag.
- 2. Mirrored Ring: Stars on the white side are orange and stars on the orange side are white, so the ring takes part in the balance too.
- 3. Stars Within: The 13-star ring placed inside the yin-yang, each star taking the color of the other half. Bold, and it holds up well at small sizes.
- 4. Seal: A round official seal: yin-yang and 13 stars at the center, with MUTUAL RESPECT and 中美相互尊重 running around the rim.
- 5. Star Dots: The two dots of the yin-yang become stars: each side carries a star of the other. The 13-star ring sits lightly around it.
- 6. First Star: Twelve white stars and one orange star at the top. The orange star is step one, freeing Eva, which the rest can follow.
- 7. 13 + 5: The right arc carries the 13 American stars, the left arc carries 5 gold stars as a nod to China's flag. Both countries share the one ring.
- 8. Free Eva Lockup: The same mark with FREE EVA as the headline and mutual respect underneath, for campaign use while Eva is still detained.
- 9. One Color: A single-color orange version for screen printing, stamps, stickers and embroidery, with no white ink needed.
