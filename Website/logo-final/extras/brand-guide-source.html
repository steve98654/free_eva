USAGE

Do

  • Use the full-color logo on black or white
  • Use single-color versions for one-ink printing (shirts, stamps, newspapers)
  • Use the Chinese lockup for Chinese-language media

Don't

  • Recolor the halves or swap 中 and US
  • Stretch, rotate or add effects
  • Shrink the north star or change the star count
  • Place on busy photos without a solid panel
