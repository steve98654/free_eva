1 Open Ring

Graduation cap inside an orange ring with a gap: a student, and the door that should open.

2 Stencil E

A solid orange block with a cut-out stencil E, in the shirt's stencil type. Reads clearly even as a tiny favicon.

3 Name Seal

A traditional Chinese name chop (印章) carved with 釋放謝翌, read top to bottom, right to left. Signals the Chinese community standing behind her.

4 Linked Rings

Two equal rings, orange and white, interlocked: China and the US bound together as equals. The mutual-respect message in one shape.

5 Two-Way Street

The ⇄ from the shirt back as its own symbol: respect has to flow both ways between China and the US.

6 Bars Bent Open

Detention bars pushed apart around a graduation cap. Says plainly that a student is being held, and that she should walk out.

7 Homecoming

A paper plane on its way into a house: she was flying home from a conference. The ask is simple, let her come home.

8 Conference Badge

A "Hello, my name is" conference lanyard badge. Points straight at the story: she was a student attending a conference.

9 Rally Button

A round pin-back campaign button: WE ARE EVA, with MUTUAL RESPECT · 中美相互尊重 arched around the rim. Made for buttons, stickers and avatars.
